Rule-Constrained Adversarial Scenario Generation

RobotX 2026 Fellow Institute for Dynamic Systems and Control (IDSC), ETH Zürich Prof. Emilio Frazzoli Jul 2026 – Aug 2026

Developed a feasibility-aware reinforcement learning framework for autonomous-vehicle safety validation. By constraining non-ego agent behaviors through feasibility learning, the framework generates realistic, safety-critical adversarial scenarios while significantly curbing unrealistic traffic-rule violations.

Synthetic Database Creation and Active Viewpoint Selection for Zebra Re-Identification

Uncertainty-guided active perception and UV texture completion for 3D animal reconstruction.

Master's Thesis · DAAD KOSPIE Scholar Institute of Flight Mechanics and Control (IFR), Universität Stuttgart Prof. Aamir Ahmad Sep 2026 – Mar 2027

Investigating synthetic database creation from 2D zebra image datasets to facilitate accurate animal re-identification. The thesis addresses core challenges in multi-view UV texture mapping, representing uncertainty across the UV texture map, and learning active viewpoint policies (single- and multi-agent) that dynamically query database views based on texture uncertainty to complete the full 3D animal reconstruction.

  • Active Perception
  • 3D Reconstruction
  • UV Texture Mapping
  • Multi-Agent Systems
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Animal Re-Identification
  • Synthetic Data

Finite-Time Centroid Tracking and Exponential Formation Stabilization in Arbitrary Swarm Configurations

Research Project Distributed Intelligence & Robotics Lab (DIRo Lab), Department of Mechanical Engineering, IIT Madras Prof. Anuj Kumar Tiwari Sep 2025 – Sep 2026

Formulated a hybrid distributed control framework for multi-robot swarms that decouples centroid tracking from formation geometry. Derived analytical proofs for global exponential formation convergence and Input-to-State Stability (ISS) under bounded disturbances via finite-time sliding-mode control.

  • Multi-Robot Systems
  • Distributed Control
  • Finite-Time Control
  • Formation Control
  • Input-to-State Stability
  • Sliding-Mode Control
