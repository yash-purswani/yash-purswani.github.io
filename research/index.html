Rule-Constrained Adversarial Scenario Generation

RobotX 2026 Fellow Institute for Dynamic Systems and Control (IDSC), ETH Zürich Prof. Emilio Frazzoli Jul 2026 – Aug 2026

  • Proposed a novel reinforcement-learning framework for generating critical yet realistic adversarial scenarios for autonomous-vehicle safety validation.
  • Reformulated adversarial scenario generation with rule-constrained non-ego agents using feasibility-aware learning.
  • Demonstrated that feasibility learning can serve as a reliable surrogate for learning and enforcing rules and constraints.
  • Extended the FREA framework with feasibility-guided RL, reducing rule violations while preserving adversarial behaviour.

Robust Finite-Time Control of Multi-Agent Swarms

Research Project Department of Mechanical Engineering, IIT Madras Prof. Anuj Kumar Tiwari Sep 2025 – Sep 2026

  • Proposed a hybrid swarm kinematic model enabling finite-time centroid tracking independent of formation geometry.
  • Proved global exponential convergence of formation errors using distance-based inter-agent coupling.
  • Established Input-to-State Stability (ISS) under bounded disturbances with analytically characterised invariant error bounds.
  • Derived super-linear disturbance attenuation for centroid tracking using finite-time sliding-mode control.
  • Multi-Robot Systems
  • Distributed Control
  • Finite-Time Control
  • Formation Control
  • Input-to-State Stability
  • Sliding-Mode Control
